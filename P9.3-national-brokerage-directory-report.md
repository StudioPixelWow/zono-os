## 11. Chunking (no 300s SIGKILL)

Hard time-budget in the cron: `HARD_LIMIT=300s`, `SAFETY_MARGIN=45s`, `SOFT_STOP=255s`, `MIN_CITY_BUDGET=60s`, `CITIES_PER_RUN=30`. A city is **only started when enough budget remains to finish it**; otherwise it is **deferred (not failed)** and — being stalest — is picked first next run (fairness, no starvation). Every invocation exits cleanly by application logic; stale-recovery stays defense-in-depth only. **Scale note:** with a live per-city actor (up to ~170s/city), the recommended production path for full national throughput is the actor's **batch/async mode** (Apify actors are not 300s-limited) — the ZONO cron triggers a batch run and ingests its dataset, decoupling the crawl duration from the serverless limit. The built per-city cron is safe today; batch mode is the throughput upgrade.
